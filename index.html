Start with your latest PSS periodic statement. Copy the Closing ABM, the three figures headed “Super salary reported on your birthday”, and the Tax-free, Taxable taxed and Taxable untaxed component dollar amounts directly from the statement. Do not convert them to percentages or add an uplift. For future ordinary-salary fields, enter ordinary salary only; the modeller applies the super-salary uplift automatically. The optional 2% top-of-range bonus is modelled as superable salary from 1 March 2027, so it does not alter a February 2027 birthday salary but does affect later birthday super salaries and FAS. Known future salaries can be entered directly, otherwise the model applies the future-growth assumption. Pension projections apply the assumed annual CPI in two compounding half-year adjustments, with the first January/July adjustment pro-rated for the period the pension has been in payment. The lifetime bonus value is an illustrative present value of the additional after-tax pension through the selected life-expectancy age, using the nominated nominal discount rate.

Take-home income over time

Fortnightly after-tax income if you keep working versus retiring on your 56th, 57th, 58th, 59th or 60th birthday. Pension lines start only when that retirement scenario begins and then include assumed CPI indexation.
The vertical marker at age 60 highlights the change in PSS pension tax treatment. Working take-home reflects the model's salary, tax and PSS contribution assumptions; pension paths use the same simplified tax-component assumptions as the rest of this prototype.
